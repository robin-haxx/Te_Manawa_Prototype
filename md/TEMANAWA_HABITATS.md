## 1. Podocarp forest

Forest Habitat screen (interglacial) · sim bands Podocarp Forest + Montane.

**Plants displayed**
- Screen: tōtara, nīkau, tawa, mamaku & ponga, kōwhai
- Sim: tōtara, kahikatea, tawa, tree ferns, nīkau, kōwhai; montane adds scattered mountain beech

**Associated bird**

- Tree ferns: shelters kiwi [I] (kiwi alive) · tallest tree fern [I] · silver fronds [I] · several similar species [I/G]
- Kōwhai:
  - seeds floated across the ocean [I/G]
  - survived the ice ages [I/G]
  - yellow flowers [I/G]
  - tūī pollinate [I/G] (tūī alive)
  - bird with two voice boxes [I/G] (tūī alive)
  - suited to cold and warm [I/G]
- Drafts (off): owl-like hearing, blue wattles, beak-tip nostrils

**Real types**
- Mixed podocarp–broadleaf forest: rimu, mātai, tōtara, kahikatea, miro emergents (30 m+) over a 9–15 m canopy of tawa and māhoe with tītoki, kāmahi, rewarewa, hīnau; kawakawa and ferns beneath
- Tōtara forest on free-draining levées and terraces: tōtara, mātai, miro, rimu, northern rātā, kōwhai
- Tawa forest on hills and the gorge: near-pure tawa, little understorey
- Northern rātā–kāmahi forest on the upper Tararua: rātā, miro, rimu over kāmahi, tawa, black maire; Hall's tōtara and *Griselinia* above ~365 m
- Glacial: beech and *Libocedrus* in shelter only; podocarps ~3% of LGM pollen

**Real types**

- Mostly river-fed swamp, plus river-margin marsh, the coastal lagoon chain, estuary saltmarsh, hill seepages
- Glacial: wetlands dammed behind dunes; interglacial highstand estuary to Shannon and Opiki

- Interglacial (pre-human): forest, open only on dry downlands, frosty terraces, sand plains, dunes; the fire-made grassland Esler describes is post-1280 CE

**Real types**
- Leatherwood (tūpari, *Olearia colensoi*) scrub above ~670 m on Arawaru, with *Coprosma*, *Myrsine divaricata*, *Dracophyllum filifolium*, *Brachyglottis*; 11 species found nowhere else in the district
- Snow tussock grassland (*Chionochloa conspicua*, *C. rubra* × *flavescens*), with wharariki
- Below: kāmahi–Hall's tōtara forest; beech to the treeline elsewhere in the region
- Glacial: treeline ~800 m lower, tops merging into the lowland shrub–grassland; only small cirque glaciers on the Tararua
- Permanent alpine habitat only ~0.95 Ma old

## Goals along the timeline

**The draw**
- One live goal is drawn at random.
- 75% of the time it comes from the current bird stage (moa → harrier → lowland → forest).
- The last 3 goals shown are skipped.
- A solve advances the stage; a soft reset restarts at moa.

**What can be live**

| Climate | Goals | Max |
|---|---|---:|
| Interglacial | 19 [I] + 20 [I/G] | 39 (measured 37–38) |
| Glacial | 13 [G] + 20 [I/G] | 33 (measured 28–31) |

The harrier has no glacial goal (tōtara is warm-only), so its stage falls through to any live goal.

**Key points**

| When | Climate | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1,000 ka, opening | interglacial | Interglacial set, minus Ōkatia and the eruption goals. Kidnappers fires at the opening instant, so it doesn't count as an eruption |
| 950 ka | glacial | First glacial set (beech, tussock, scrub, *Dracophyllum*) |
| 900 ka, Kaukatea | glacial | Eruption goals join: grows back after a volcano [G], spreads after ash falls [I/G] |
| 859–818 ka | interglacial | Out-competes nīkau drops out where nīkau is scarce |
| 1,000–350 ka | both | Subalpine ground grows ~450 → ~8,300 cells (gate 200); *Dracophyllum* and wharariki goals live throughout (one seed, 1920×1080) |
| 400 ka | — | Ōkatia's date gate opens (needs an interglacial with ≥10 grown tōtara; first seen 342–314 ka) |
| 349 ka, Whakamaru | glacial | No change (eruption gate already open) |
| 122 ka | interglacial | Full interglacial set |
| 25.5 ka, LGM / Ōruanui | glacial | Full glacial set |

Bird-gated goals (kererū + kōkako, kiwi, tūī) go dark only if those birds die out; the refound backstop normally prevents it.
